GamStop – GamStop is a free, independent tool that allows UK players to self-exclude from all online gambling sites regulated by the UK Gambling Commission. While top UK online casinos offer a wide variety of secure banking options, some payment methods are strictly off-limits due to regulatory guidelines. Here are the key options you won’t see supported:

Cheques – Once a common method, cheques have become obsolete in the online gambling space. The slow processing times and outdated format have led to their complete removal from all major UK platforms.

Cryptocurrencies – Digital currencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum are not allowed under current UKGC rules. While they are gaining popularity globally, UK-licensed casinos do not support crypto transactions at this time.

Pay-by-Phone – Previously seen as a convenient mobile solution, pay-by-phone billing has largely disappeared from the UK casino scene due to increased regulatory scrutiny and limited practicality.
